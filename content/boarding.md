Overnight stays in our home, not a kennel. Boarding runs across a stretch of days, so instead of picking a time slot you tell us the dates:

1. Submit the form with your stay dates and your preferred drop-off and pick-up windows.
2. We check the calendar and confirm by email — usually within a day.
3. Once approved, your stay is on our schedule and you get a confirmation with drop-off details.

Vaccination records are required before the first stay — complete the [new pup form](../new-pup/) if that hasn't happened yet.
