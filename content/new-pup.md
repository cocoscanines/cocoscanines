---
title: "New Pup Form"
description: "Intake form for new dogs at Coco's Canines — pup details, vaccination records upload, and liability waiver signature."
---

Before your dog's first visit, tell us about them. The form takes about five minutes and covers:

- **Your pup** — name, age, breed, temperament, medications, and any quirks we should know
- **Vaccination proof** — upload photos or PDFs of current records (rabies, DHPP, bordetella); 10 MB per file is plenty for phone photos
- **Liability waiver** — read the [current waiver](../waiver/), then check the agreement box and sign on the form

We review everything before your first day and reply if anything's missing.
